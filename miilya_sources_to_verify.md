# Mi'ilya — Sources Still to Verify

**Status: unverified.** Nothing on this list is in the database as evidence — these are pointers to check, not confirmed facts. A source moves off this list only once someone has actually read it and it's been written into `evidence_records` as `verified` or `lead`.

Organized by which stage each source most likely touches, based on Maya's own bibliography description — not on having read the source directly.

---

## Stage 4 — Historical Depth & Continuity

- **Shukri Arraf, *Mi'ilya* (book, 563 pages)** — described as the single most comprehensive local history of the village. Written by a member of one of the three founding families (Arraf) documented in Khamisy's own academic work. Likely the highest-value single source left unprocessed, precisely because it's too long to summarize secondhand — worth reading directly if a copy can be obtained.
- Guérin (1880), Mariti (1792), *Palestine Under the Moslems* — additional 18th/19th-century primary travel/geography accounts referenced in the bibliography but not directly read.
- Hütteroth & Abdulfattah (1977), *Historical Geography of Palestine* — the secondary analysis of Ottoman tax registers; we've used a *citation* of the 1555/6 register, not this source directly.

## Stage 8 — Community & Intangible Values

- **Afifa Mahoul Hamisa's referenced book**, *معليا على طبق الذاكرة* (roughly "Mi'ilya on a Plate of Memory") — her own memoir piece points to this as a deeper source on the village's economic/agricultural geography. Promising lead, not yet located or read.
- The remaining local Arabic-language pieces (Ahlan.co.il local history and political-history articles) — useful mainly for oral memory, terminology, and place names per Maya's own quality assessment; worth a pass specifically to cross-check names/dates against the academic record, not to introduce new claims on their own.
- **A real gap this list can't fill**: none of this is a substitute for actually interviewing the Assaf, Arraf, and Qassis families directly. Every source here is secondhand; a real FPIC conversation and a real knowledge-holder count/age-profile survey require direct outreach, not more reading.

## Stage 14 — Boundaries & GIS

- **Khirbet Balaton** — a specific place name tied to farmer's roads and olive groves, mentioned in a Palestinian village encyclopedia entry. Worth locating precisely and adding as a real `map_features` entry once actual coordinates are available — currently just a name, not mappable data.

## Stage 13 — Economic Sustainability

- Tourism/hospitality initiative pieces (Mako 2022, Amiram Oren's photo-documentary) — mention active hospitality ventures around the castle and winery; worth a direct check on current operating status rather than relying on 2022-era tourism writing.

## General / lower priority

- Several remaining local and international press pieces (Ynet ×2, DOMRADIO in German, Photour, Avnei Moreshet, Encyclopedia.com) mostly **confirm** what's already independently verified from academic sources, rather than adding new specific facts. Worth a quick pass if time allows, but not urgent — the load-bearing claims in the database don't depend on them.

## How this list should be used

When any of these get read properly: extract the specific claim, tag it `verified` (primary/official source, read directly) or `lead` (secondary, still needs confirmation), link it to the specific question it answers, and it moves out of this document into the actual database. This file should shrink over time, not grow.
